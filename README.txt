How it works:
1. Customer opens Customize.
2. Customer selects budget.
3. Products above the budget are hidden.
4. Customer selects products.
5. Total is calculated.
6. Customer enters name, phone, occasion, quantity and delivery date.
7. Customer can send the enquiry to WhatsApp or Email.

To deploy on Netlify:
Drag the entire folder into Netlify Drop, or upload the ZIP.
